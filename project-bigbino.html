Bigbino Engine

Recruits engine — machining & assembly

Overview

The Toothless rocket and Bigbino hybrid engine was built as part of a recruits campaign with eight team members. I worked on the design of the hybrid engine, machined and supported assembly, integration and hotfire testing.

Design

The Bigbino hybrid engine uses an Urbanski-Colburn valve with a floating piston design. The tank is filled through a push fitting and the ignitor is triggered with e-matches, which burns through the fill tube and opens the tank.

The tank is filled through a push fitting and the ignitor is triggered with e-matches, which burn through a burst tube and open the tank at ignition. I designed the floating piston so injector plates could be swapped between tests, allowing the team to trial both vortex and showerhead injector types.
