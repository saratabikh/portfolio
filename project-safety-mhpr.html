Safety in Monash High Powered Rocketry

Safety Lead • July 2025 – July 2026

Safety Representative • July 2024 – July 2025

Scope

As Safety Lead at Monash High Powered Rocketry, I managed a safety team of 15 people and focused on the development of a team-wide safety culture. My work centred on standardising our safety documentation and integrating safety gates into design reviews. By proactively considering safety early in the development cycle, these improvements enabled safer manufacturing and testing while saving engineers time and streamlining their overall design process.

Process Improvements

Standardised Risk Assessments and Safe Work Instructions

Revamped legacy safety procedures by creating and reviewing over 50 Risk Assessments (RAs) and Safe Work Instructions (SWIs) to improve documentation across the team.

Safety Gates in Design Reviews

Introduced safety gates in design reviews so hazards and mitigations were considered early in the engineering process, supporting a proactive approach across 150+ members.

Event Risk Management

Organised safety logistics for testing, outreach, and launch events by developing Event Risk Management Plans and overseeing team execution to ensure safety of all attendees.

High-Risk Testing & Events

I managed safety of flightline members and over 70 spectators during our team launches of Project Meridian. In the lead up to large team events like this one, I managed safety logistics, organising, reviewing and getting our safety documentation approved in time for each testing event, so the team could test safely.

Impact

Shifting the Team's Safety Culture

When I stepped into the role, I championed a shift towards proactive safety management. Members began independently evaluating the safety of their designs, and risk assessment was started early in any project's development rather than at the end.

Developing Leadership Skills

Leading the safety team started my journey of learning how to manage people, and gave me experience in how to transfer knowledge, delegate, communicate clearly, and keep a group of busy, passionate members aligned, supported and accountable.

Growing My Team

One of my favourite experiences was teaching my team, who started with little to no safety knowledge. By the end of their year, they could confidently create documentation themselves and independently help other members of the team, I was very proud of them!
