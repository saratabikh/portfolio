Safety in Monash High Powered Rocketry

Safety Lead • July 2025 – Present

Safety Representative • July 2024 – July 2025

Scope

As Safety Lead at Monash High Powered Rocketry, I manage a dedicated safety team of 15 people and focus on the development of a team-wide safety culture. My work centres on standardising safety documentation, integrating safety gates into design reviews, and enabling safe manufacturing and testing.

Process Improvements

Standardised Risk Assessments and Safe Work Instructions

Revamped legacy safety procedures by creating Risk Assessments (RAs) and Safe Work Instructions (SWIs) to improve documentation across the team.

Safety Gates in Design Reviews

Introduced safety gates in design reviews so hazards and mitigations are considered early in the engineering process, supporting a proactive approach across 150+ members.

Event Risk Management

Organised safety logistics for testing, outreach, and launch events by developing Event Risk Management Plans and overseeing team execution to ensure safety of all attendees.

High-Risk Testing & Events

I acted as the safety representative for our highest-risk activities, including hotfires, launches and large team events — organising, reviewing and getting our safety documentation approved in time for each testing event. I developed and reviewed detailed risk assessments and safe work instructions in collaboration with part designers and academic advisors, and managed safety logistics so the team could test safely.

Impact

Team Award

My contribution as a Safety Representative safety improvements supported safe testing of advanced propulsion systems and contributed to the team receiving the Jim Furfaro Award for Technical Excellence among 140+ student teams.

Learning to Manage a Team

Leading the safety team taught me how to genuinely manage people — how to transfer knowledge,delegate, communicate clearly, and keep a group of busy, passionate members aligned, supported and accountable.

Growing My Team

One of my favourite experiences was teaching my team, who started with little to no safety knowledge. By the end of their year, they could confidently create documentation themselves and independently help other members of the team — I'm very proud of them!
